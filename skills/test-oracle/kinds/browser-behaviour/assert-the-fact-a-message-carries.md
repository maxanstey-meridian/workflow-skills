## Assert the fact a message carries

**Law:** Assert the fact a message carries (a number, a name, which question it is on), not its sentence, unless the source gives the sentence.

**When:** Asserting anything a page shows as a message.

Bad: Asserting the exact sentence the component happens to render for a refused term length.
Good: Asserting the refused record's name and the day its term ends, the facts the row's `Expected` names.
