## Disproving a claim about a page

**Law:** For "a person can't cause this from a page" (a `CantHappen` claim), the disprover tries to find a way: the page opened by its address, a page loaded before the state changed, a second tab, a second person, Back, a reload, an upload, a pasted value.

**When:** Checking a row's `CantHappen` claim.

Where a control is hidden, the claim says what hides it, and whether opening by address, or acting from a page loaded before the state changed, still reaches it.

Bad: A `CantHappen` claim about a suspended record's amend control, checked by looking only at the page in the tab that made the suspension.
Good: The claim checked from a page opened by its address, from the page loaded before the suspension, and from a second tab signed in as another admin, each way named in the verdict.
