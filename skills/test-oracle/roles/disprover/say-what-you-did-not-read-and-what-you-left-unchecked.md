## Say what you did not read, and what you left unchecked

**Preference:** Say what you did not read, and what you checked only partly, in your report's "Not checked" section, each with the reason and what would settle it.

**When:** The end of your grading.

Bad: A report silent about the cited source file it never opened and the two files it opened only partly.
Good: The same report with "Not checked" naming each, so the gap is visible.
