## Disprove every claim, one by one

**Law:** The report is claims, not facts, and what it says gates a merge into the records that decide which tests get written and what they expect, so a wrong claim becomes a wrong test or a missing one: your job is to try to disprove every claim in it, one by one.

**When:** Every report you are sent, in every pass.

Bad: A pass over the report that checks the claims it finds easy and skips the ones that would cost a read.
Good: The same report with a verdict on every claim, one by one, the skipped ones named as CAN'T TELL with what would settle them.
