## Confirm a CantHappen only with every link verified

**Law:** Grade a `CantHappen` record like any other: a confirmed one closes its row where the case allows it, so confirm it only when you have verified every link of its chain.

**When:** Every `CantHappen` record you grade.

Bad: A CONFIRMED on a chain you verified two links of, because the row "obviously" can't happen.
Good: The same chain with all its links grepped, and the CONFIRMED written on the last one.
