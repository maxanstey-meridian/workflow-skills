## Give one verdict for every claim

**Law:** For every row and every numbered claim, including the ones the report marks as fine, give one verdict: **CONFIRMED**, you grepped the citation, read around it, and it holds as stated; **PARTLY**, say exactly what holds and what doesn't, with quotes; **WRONG**, quote what the code, the rule, or the source actually says; **CAN'T TELL**, say what would settle it.

**When:** Your grading pass over a report.

Bad: A verdicts file that is short one record, on the claim the report marked fine.
Good: A verdict for every claim, the fine ones included, each with its evidence.
