## Grep the quote in a cited rule

**Law:** On a claim that "a written rule says …", grep the quote in the rule, and ask whether that is what it says, or the agent's reading of it.

**When:** Every claim that cites a written source or a Meridian rule.

Bad: A CONFIRMED on a rule cited for a sentence the rule doesn't hold, because the quote was the agent's paraphrase.
Good: The same citation grepped, the rule's actual sentence quoted back, and the verdict on the gap between them.
