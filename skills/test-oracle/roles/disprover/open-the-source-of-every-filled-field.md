## Open the source of every filled field

**Law:** For a fill report, for every field the filler wrote — the fields of the pass — open the source it rests on: does that source say that, for that row? Does every `CantHappen` record cite every link of its chain? A field that says more, or less, than its source does is WRONG.

**When:** A fill report, for every field it filled.

Bad: A CONFIRMED on a field whose source says one thing and the fill says a rounded version of it.
Good: The same field with the source opened, the rounding quoted on both sides, and the verdict PARTLY — what holds, what doesn't.
