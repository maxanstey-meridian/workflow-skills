## Name the grep behind every verdict

**Preference:** Every verdict names the grep you ran and what it returned, with quotes for anything not CONFIRMED, and no recommendations, no options, and no asks.

**When:** Every line of your verdicts file, and every line of your report.

Bad: An Evidence line that says "checked" with no grep and no return.
Good: The same line with the grep, its result, and the quote that holds or breaks.
