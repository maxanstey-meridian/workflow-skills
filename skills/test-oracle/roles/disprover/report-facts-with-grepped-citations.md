## Report facts with grepped citations

**Preference:** Your report is facts only, with no recommendations, no options, and no asks, and every claim in your report and every verdict in your verdicts file names the grep you ran and what it returned, carries its citation — a file plus a verbatim quote, grepped — and quotes the record for every finding, with quotes for anything not CONFIRMED: every claim is checked by grepping the citation it names, and there is no second tier of claim.

**When:** Every line of your report, and every line of your verdicts file.

Bad: An Evidence line that says "the filler's claim seems right" or "checked", with no grep and no return.
Good: The same line with the grep, its result, the quote that holds or breaks, and the verdict on them.
