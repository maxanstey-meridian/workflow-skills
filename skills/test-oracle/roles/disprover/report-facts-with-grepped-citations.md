## Report facts with grepped citations

**Preference:** Your report is facts only: every claim carries its citation — a file plus a verbatim quote, grepped, with the grep's result — and the record quoted for every finding, with no recommendations, no options, and no asks; every claim is checked by grepping the citation it names, and there is no second tier of claim.

**When:** Every line of your report.

Bad: "The filler's claim seems right" as a line, with no grep and no record quoted.
Good: The same claim with the grep's result, the record quoted, and the verdict on them.
