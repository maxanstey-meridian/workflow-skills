## Search for a source before agreeing there is none

**Law:** On a question record, or a claim of "no written source" or "no source", search the docs, specs, and contracts under the root, the rulings files (the top-level `rulings.md` and the slice's), and the `rules-*.md` files you read first, before you agree: agree only when none of them answers the field, and the code's own say is unclear or contradicts itself, with nothing plainly wrong in it; in your own verdicts and report, invent no intent — where no source in the order answers, say "no source" and supply none.

**When:** Every question record, every claim of no source, and every claim about what a row should do.

Bad: A CONFIRMED on "no source" with the spec folder unsearched, where the spec answers the field on page one.
Good: The same field with the search done, the spec's sentence cited, and the question refused — WRONG, with the source.
