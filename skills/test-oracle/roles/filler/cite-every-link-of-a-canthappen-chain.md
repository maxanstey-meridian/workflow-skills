## Cite every link of a CantHappen chain

**Law:** `CantHappen` is the cited chain that shows the row can't happen, and a confirmed one closes the row, so you fill it only where it holds, only as a value, and every link of the chain carries its citation: if any link lacks one, or rests on a file you didn't open in full, you are not sure, and you write none.

**When:** A row you believe cannot happen.

Bad: A `CantHappen` whose middle link is "the validator stops it", with the validator unread.
Good: The same chain with the validator opened in full and quoted, link by link, to the row's `Where`.
