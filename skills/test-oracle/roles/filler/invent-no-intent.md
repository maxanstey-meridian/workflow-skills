## Invent no intent

**Law:** Don't invent intent: an expected value is a quoted source with its `Source` word — a written source, the user's words from a rulings file with their date, a Meridian rule, the code's own say labelled `today`, or an obvious wrong with its `Why` — and with none of these, it is a question for the user.

**When:** Every expected field you write.

Bad: "The product surely wants the list sorted by date" on a field no source says to sort.
Good: The same field as a question, with the one recommended answer, until a source or a ruling says.
