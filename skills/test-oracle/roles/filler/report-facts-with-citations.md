## Report facts with citations

**Preference:** Your report is facts only — what you read, the citation, what the code does — with no recommendations, no options, and no asks, and every claim carries its citation: a file plus a verbatim quote that greps. There is no second tier of claim.

**When:** Every line of your report.

Bad: "I think the row is fine" as a report line, with nothing to grep.
Good: "The row's value greps in the spec at the cited sentence" — the file, the quote, and what you read around it.
