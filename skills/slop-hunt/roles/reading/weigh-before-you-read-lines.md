## Weigh before you read lines

**Default:** Before reading file by file, list what each piece of your scope is for and its line count from `sizes.txt`, ask what it gives users, what the cheapest shape is (the platform, a library, the host's or caller's job, a smaller design, or nothing), and what users would lose, then read the files to confirm or overturn that weighing in your weight map.

**When:** Starting a hunter or design-reader pass over an area or repository.

Bad: Diving straight into line 1 of the first alphabetical file and flagging small nits before understanding what the 1,200-line folder exists to do.
Good: Building the weight map from `sizes.txt` first, then reading the files to verify or overturn each piece's earned-vs-slop case.
