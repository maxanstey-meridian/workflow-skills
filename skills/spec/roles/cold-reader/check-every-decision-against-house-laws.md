## Check every Decision against house Laws

**Law:** Flag any Decision or piece that breaks a house Law or rule in `findings[]` (naming the rule), populate `laws[]` in `cold-read.yaml` with one entry per bearing Law for each Decision in `spec.decisions` (`status: kept` with `lawPath` and `keptBy` stating concrete evidence from the code, platform, or spec proving how the Law is satisfied — never a verbatim copy of the Decision's `why` — or `status: broken` with `lawPath` and its 1-based `findingIndex` into `findings[]`), or a single `status: none-bears` (a procedural Law such as `coding/record-why-no-library-fits-before-hand-rolling.md` never substitutes for the substantive Law `coding/use-a-maintained-library-before-building-machinery.md`), and check every `pieces[].change` for hand-rolled general-purpose machinery (parsers, schema/spec validators, serializers, clients, schedulers, reflection engines) recording each in `machinery[]` as either `backed-by-decision` (linked to a Decision checked under `coding/use-a-maintained-library-before-building-machinery.md`) or `unbacked` (with a `findings[]` entry flagging the missing Decision and library check), or `machinery: []` when none does.
